## Joseph Smarrelli

I build basketball analytics from public data: shot charts, play-by-play, and
the lineups you have to rebuild yourself from substitution logs. Every project
here runs from raw files, commits the table behind every number it quotes, and
says plainly what its data cannot see.

Six years running operations and analytics on the founding team of Songlorious,
and seven seasons as a volunteer head basketball coach, with four straight
league titles. Based in Atlanta.

**[Read the write-ups →](https://jmsmarrelli.github.io/basketball-analytics/)**

### Selected work

| | |
|---|---|
| [**spurs-game-management**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/spurs-game-management) *(pin)* | San Antonio's 2025-26 season through three coaching questions: what a stolen-ball turnover costs in the next 12 seconds, whether timeouts stop runs, and how to play the last 40 seconds of a quarter. |
| [**west-playoff-scouting**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/west-playoff-scouting) *(pin)* | Opponent scouting for the Western contenders. A quarter of Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's points never show up on a shot chart. |
| [**spurs-research**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/spurs-research) *(pin)* | Rotations rebuilt from substitution logs. Opponents' rim share falls 4.6 points during Wembanyama's minutes, compared within the same games. |
| [**nba-rapm**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/nba-rapm) *(pin)* | Regularized adjusted plus-minus, built by rebuilding the ten players on the floor for 11,145 games. |
| [**nba-shot-value**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/nba-shot-value) *(pin)* | An expected-points model that splits any set of attempts into the shots you generated and the shots you made. |
| [**contender-shot-quality**](https://github.com/jmsmarrelli/contender-shot-quality) *(pin)* | Shot quality is readable in a week. Shot making takes most of a season, and a playoff series is not a season. |

The rest, including studies of the Spurs' rest policy, the Rodeo Road Trip,
Tim Duncan's defense and the 2026 Finals, are in the
[index](https://jmsmarrelli.github.io/basketball-analytics/).

**Tools:** Python (pandas, scikit-learn, scipy, matplotlib), R, SQL.
**Data:** the NBA's public shot chart and play-by-play feeds and ESPN's public
box scores. No proprietary or tracking data.
